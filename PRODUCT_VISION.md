ADOps AI

Enterprise Identity Operations Platform

Mission

Transform Active Directory operational data into actionable intelligence through automation, evidence correlation, AI-assisted analysis, and intuitive visualization.

Vision

Reduce incident investigation time while improving the quality, consistency, and explainability of Root Cause Analysis.

Objectives

• Automate evidence collection
• Correlate information from multiple sources
• Generate explainable RCA
• Visualize Active Directory health
• Build an organizational knowledge platform
• Reduce Mean Time to Resolution (MTTR)
